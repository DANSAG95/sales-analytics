# Sales Analysis

Sales analysis using SQL and Power BI to identify trends, top-performing products, and revenue drivers.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales performance of a technology and electronics retailer operating across 8 countries, with 66 physical stores and an online sales channel.

The analysis combines SQL and Power BI to evaluate business performance and identify the products, categories, markets, and stores that have the biggest impact on revenue and profitability.

## Business Questions

### Overall Performance

* What are the overall sales, revenue, cost, profit, and profit margin?

### Product Performance

* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which products generate the most profit?
* Which products have the lowest profit?

### Market Performance

* Which countries generate the most revenue and profit?
* Which product categories perform best in each country?
* Which products perform best in each country?

### Store Performance

* How does store size relate to revenue and profitability?

## Key Insights

* The business generated **$55.76M in revenue** and **$32.66M in profit**, resulting in an overall **58.58% profit margin**.

* The **United States** is by far the largest market, generating approximately **$29.87M in revenue** and **$17.49M in profit**. However, profit margins are relatively consistent across all countries.

* **Computers** is the strongest category by both revenue and total profit, while **Music, Movies and Audio Books** has the highest profit margin at **60.98%**.

* Larger physical stores generate substantially more revenue and profit on average than smaller stores. However, profit margins remain relatively similar across store sizes.

* Product performance varies significantly across markets. **Computers and TV and Video products frequently appear among the top-performing products within individual countries**, while some products generate very little profit and may require further review.

## Tools

* **SQL (PostgreSQL)** — Data preparation, aggregation and analysis
* **Power BI** — Data visualisation and dashboard development
